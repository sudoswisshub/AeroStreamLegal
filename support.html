Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app work without signal?

Yes for GPS position, coordinates, the compass, route recording, Backtrack, saved places and the field and aviation tools. Map backgrounds and worldwide place search use Apple Maps and need a connection; without one, the map may be blank but your position, tracks and tools still work.

Why does the app ask for location access?

To show your coordinates, record routes and guide you. "While Using" is enough for recording, including with the screen locked. "Always" is only needed if you turn on arrival alerts, which notify you when the app is closed.

My recording was interrupted. Is it lost?

No. The app saves an in-progress recording regularly. After a crash or restart, it offers to resume the recording or save it as a track.

Does the SOS beacon call for help?

No. It flashes the screen and torch and plays a sound so people nearby can find you, and it can prepare a text message with your position for your emergency contacts. It does not contact emergency services. Call your local emergency number (112 / 911) or use your phone's Emergency SOS via satellite.

Can I rely on the aviation tools?

They are planning aids, not certified equipment. Always verify against your aircraft's POH, official charts and publications, current weather and ATC. The built-in airport list is a small sample set.

How does Peer Radar share my position?

Only with nearby devices that use the same group code, directly over Wi-Fi/Bluetooth. Stop Peer Radar to stop sharing.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

In Settings, use Export Library Backup, then import it on the new device. GPX files can also be imported and exported for tracks.

What does Pro include?

Pro supports continued development of the app. Safety features are never locked behind Pro. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings.
