2. Description of the App

The App shows your GPS position and coordinates, records and imports routes, stores places and collections, and provides offline field, outdoor, aviation-planning and safety tools (for example E6B, weight & balance, glide, fuel, holding, terrain clearance, sun and weather estimates, wilderness first-aid reference, and an SOS light/sound beacon). Most features work without a network connection; map backgrounds and place search use Apple Maps and need a connection.

3. Not for Navigation, Flight or Medical Decisions

Important: The App is a planning and reference aid. It is not certified aviation, navigation, medical or emergency equipment.

  • Aviation tools produce estimates only. Always use your aircraft's Pilot's Operating Handbook, official charts and publications, NOTAMs, current weather and ATC. The built-in airport data is a small sample set and may be outdated.
  • Wilderness medicine content is general reference, not medical advice. Seek professional medical help whenever possible.
  • Ballistics and other field calculators are estimates for education and planning.
  • GPS readings can be inaccurate or unavailable. Carry a map and compass in the backcountry.
  • SOS features do not contact emergency services. The SOS beacon only flashes the screen and torch and plays a sound on your device, and SOS text messages are sent through your own Messages app. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (such as 112 or 911) or use your phone's emergency satellite features.
